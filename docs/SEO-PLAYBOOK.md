# SEO playbook — getting Mahadev Overseas to the top in Guntur

Nobody can guarantee position #1 on Google. What reliably gets a local consultancy to the top of searches like **"study visa consultants near me"**, **"best overseas education consultancy in Guntur"** or **"Canada study visa consultant Guntur"** is three things working together:

| Factor | What Google looks at | Who does it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relevance** | Does the website clearly match the search? | ✅ Done on the website (see below) |
| **Distance** | How close the office is to the searcher ("near me") | Google Business Profile address |
| **Prominence** | Reviews, ratings, links, mentions across the web | You — ongoing (steps below) |

For "near me" searches, the **map pack** (the 3 businesses shown on the map) is decided mostly by your **Google Business Profile**, not the website. That is the #1 priority.

## Week 1 — must do

### 1. Google Business Profile (biggest impact for "near me")
Go to **business.google.com** and claim/verify "Mahadev Overseas".

- **Primary category:** *Educational consultant*. Additional: *Visa consulting service*, *Immigration & naturalization service* (only if you offer it), *Education center* / *Coaching center* (for IELTS/PTE).
- **Name:** exactly "Mahadev Overseas" — don't add keywords to the name (Google can suspend you for it).
- **Address:** exactly as on the website, e.g. `Door No 21-6-139, Near Ramalayam, Sakethapuram, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh 522007`. Fix the same text in Admin → Contact details so they **match character for character** (the saved one has `21_6-139` and stray spaces before commas).
- **Phone:** +91 91217 74843 (same as website). **Website:** `https://www.mahadevoverseas.services`.
- **Hours:** Mon–Sat 10:00–18:00 (same as website). Add holiday hours when closed.
- **Services:** add each service with a short description (Admissions, Student visa, SOP & LOR, IELTS/PTE coaching, Education loans, Accommodation, Pre-departure, Dependent visas) and each country.
- **Description (750 chars):** who you help, which countries, Guntur & nearby towns, free counselling. No promises of visas.
- **Photos:** 10+ real photos — office exterior (helps people find you), signboard, interior, team, counselling sessions, students with offer letters/visas (with consent). Add 2–3 new photos every month.
- **Map pin:** check it's exactly on your office. Then copy the coordinates (right-click the pin in Google Maps) into Admin → Contact details → **Geo**.
- **Posts:** one post per week (intake deadlines, IELTS batch dates, visa success with consent, offers).
- **Q&A:** add your own top questions and answers (reuse the website FAQ).
- **Messaging / WhatsApp** link: turn on and reply fast — response time counts.

### 2. Google reviews (biggest impact for "best consultancy")
- In your Business Profile click **Ask for reviews**, copy the link, and paste it into Admin → Contact details → **Google review url** (shows "Review us on Google" on the site).
- Ask **every** happy student and parent — right after the offer letter, the visa, and when they land. Send the link on WhatsApp.
- Aim for a steady flow (e.g. 4–8 a month) rather than a burst. Reply to **every** review, politely, including negative ones.
- Never buy reviews, review-swap, or offer gifts for reviews — Google removes them and can penalise the profile.

### 3. Search Console (verified ✅)
- **Sitemaps** → submit `sitemap.xml`.
- **URL inspection** → paste the home page URL → **Request indexing**. Repeat for Countries, Services, Contact, and each new guide.
- Check **Pages** and **Core Web Vitals** monthly; fix anything marked "Poor" or "Not indexed".

## Every month — content and links

- **Publish 2–4 guides a month** (Admin → Guides (blog)). Answer real questions students ask you, for example:
  - Cost of studying in Canada / UK / Australia / Germany from India (a draft is ready — add verified figures, then publish)
  - IELTS vs PTE: which is better for you
  - Student visa documents checklist for each country
  - Best courses for Indian students in Ireland / Germany
  - September vs January intake — which to choose
  - Spouse/dependent visa rules by country
  - Student life stories from Guntur students abroad (with consent and photos)
- Put the city and the main keyword naturally in the title and first paragraph; never stuff keywords.
- **Links:** get mentioned by partner universities (agent/partner pages), local colleges and coaching centres, Guntur news sites, education fairs and seminars you attend, and local business associations.
- **Social:** share each guide and success story on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, and your Business Profile posts.

## What to avoid
- Promising visa or loan approval ("100% visa", "guaranteed loan") — misleading, and the site's tests block it.
- Keyword-stuffed business names, fake addresses, multiple profiles for one office.
- Copying text from other websites; thin pages made just for keywords.
- Buying links or reviews.

## How long it takes
New sites and profiles usually start appearing for local searches within **4–12 weeks** once the Business Profile is verified and reviews start coming in. Top positions for competitive phrases ("best consultancy") take **3–6+ months** of steady reviews, guides and links. Track progress in Search Console (queries & positions) and Business Profile (calls, direction requests, website clicks).
